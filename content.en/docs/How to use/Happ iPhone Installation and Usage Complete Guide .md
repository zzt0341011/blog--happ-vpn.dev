# Happ iPhone Installation and Usage Complete Guide

## 1. What is Happ? Why Do You Need a US Apple ID?

**Happ** (full name: Happ - Proxy Utility) is a free proxy client based on the Xray core. It supports mainstream protocols including VLESS (Reality), VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, and Hysteria2. It does not provide any server nodes itself — it is only a tool. You need to import an airport subscription link to use it.

Since the App Store in many countries usually cannot find this app, you need a **US (or other foreign region) Apple ID** to download it from the App Store. The most common practice is to temporarily use a shared US Apple ID.

![happ-1001.jpg](https://happ-vpn.dev/img/happ-1001.jpg)

### Important Reminders (Must Read)

- A shared Apple ID should **only** be used to log in to the App Store for downloading apps.
- **Never** log in with a shared account in “Settings → Top Apple ID / iCloud”, otherwise it may trigger Lost Mode and remotely lock your phone, which can only be fixed by restoring the device.
- After downloading, immediately sign out of the shared account and switch back to your own account.
- Shared accounts may become invalid at any time or be used by multiple people simultaneously. It is recommended to switch back to your own ID as soon as the download is complete.

## 5. Daily Usage Tips

- You can test node latency (Ping) inside Happ and prioritize nodes with low latency and high stability
- Supports rule-based routing (can be configured as needed)
- It is recommended to enable practical features such as “Start on Boot” (depending on the version)
- When the subscription link is updated, simply refresh the subscription in Happ
- If all nodes fail, first check whether the airport has expired or the subscription has been reset

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: The shared ID shows account abnormality / requires verification after login?**  
A: Switch to a new shared account. Shared accounts are often used by many people and may be risk-controlled.

**Q: Can’t find Happ after downloading?**  
A: Confirm the developer is Flyfrog LLC to avoid downloading a counterfeit app.

**Q: Connected but cannot access the internet?**  
A: Check whether the subscription is valid, whether the nodes are blocked, whether the correct protocol is enabled, or try switching to other nodes.

**Q: Can I use a shared ID long-term?**  
A: Not recommended. Shared IDs carry higher risks. For long-term use, it is better to register your own US Apple ID (free, only requires an email and US address information).
