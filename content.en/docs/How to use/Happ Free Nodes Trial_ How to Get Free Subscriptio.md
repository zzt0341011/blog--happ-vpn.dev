## 4. FAQ

### Are Happ free nodes really free?

Distinguish between the client and the node service. Happ is the client; free nodes or trial subscriptions are provided by third parties. Whether they are free, require registration, or have traffic limits depends on the actual activity rules.

### Can I connect immediately after claiming the free trial?

Usually you still need to import the obtained valid subscription or configuration into Happ, then select an available node and establish a connection.

### Why doesn’t the free trial have the region I want?

Trial plans may open only partial nodes. Check the activity description; if you need more regions, compare paid plans that include the corresponding routes.

### How is the speed of the free trial?

Performance can vary by node and network environment. It is recommended to actually test connection latency, download speed and stability instead of judging solely by free or paid status.

### Must I buy after the trial ends?

Not necessarily. You can decide based on your own needs. Before purchasing, confirm price, validity period, traffic limits and refund rules.

### Which protocols does Happ support?

Commonly supported protocols include VLESS, VMess, Shadowsocks, Trojan, SOCKS and Hysteria2. The actual support range is subject to the official description of the current app version.

### What should I check before purchasing?

Confirm node regions, subscription compatibility, traffic rules, validity period, device limits, privacy policy and after-sales terms.
